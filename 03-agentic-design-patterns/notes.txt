How to design good ai agents:

What makes a good agent:
    - Space: environment agent is working in
        connecting events, people, knowledge
        easily discoverable
        found in UI/UX of application
    - Time: This is how the agent operates overtime with a human
        Learns overtime
        Connects to past events and enabling reflection design pattern
        Show users past promopts and interaction with UI
    - Core: Embracing uncertainty
        allowing LLMs to create/act on plans
        enabling trust and transparency
        Visibly controls and feedback tools
